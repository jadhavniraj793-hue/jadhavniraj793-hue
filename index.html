The desk where it happens — 2:47 AM

I turn polygons into stories.

Hey — I’m Niraj, a self-taught 3D artist & motion designer from India. What began as curiosity about how games were made turned into a full-blown obsession with making still frames move.

My toolkit is Blender, After Effects, Illustrator & Figma. I love owning the whole pipeline — blocking, look-dev, animation, lighting — and yes, the 47th render attempt at 2 AM.

Right now I’m open to freelance work and junior roles in 3D & motion. If you have a world that needs building, say hi.
